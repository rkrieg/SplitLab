Work comp questions

Workers' Comp Questions Georgia Workers Ask

What is workers' comp?

Workers' compensation means you've sustained an injury at work. It is meant to cover your medical treatment and part of your pay while you cannot work.

Is workmans comp the same as workers' compensation?

Yes. People say workmans comp, workers comp, and work comp. They all mean the same thing.

How much does a workers comp lawyer cost?

Nothing upfront. Darwin works on a contingency fee, so you pay only if we win.

Do I need a job injury attorney if my claim was already accepted?

Often, yes. An accepted claim can still be delayed, reduced, or cut off.

What if my employer says it was my fault?

Georgia workers' compensation is generally a no-fault system, which means fault is usually not the question.

How long do I have to file?

Georgia sets deadlines for reporting your injury and filing your claim, and they are shorter than most people expect. Call and give Darwin your dates.
