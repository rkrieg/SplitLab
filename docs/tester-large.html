Frequently asked questions

How long does implementation take?

Most teams are fully connected within a day. Larger enterprise rollouts with custom permissioning typically take one to two weeks.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes, every plan starts with a 14-day free trial and no credit card is required to sign up.

What integrations do you support?

We support over 40 native integrations including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Zendesk, Stripe, and Segment, plus a general-purpose webhook API.

Is my data secure?

Synapse is SOC 2 Type II certified and encrypts data at rest and in transit. Enterprise plans include dedicated data residency options.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your billing settings with no cancellation fees.

Do you support single sign-on?

SSO and SCIM provisioning are available on the Enterprise plan, supporting Okta, Azure AD, and any SAML 2.0 provider.

What kind of support is included?

Starter includes email support with a 24-hour response time. Growth adds priority support, and Enterprise includes a dedicated customer success manager.

Can I import historical data?

Yes, our onboarding team can help backfill historical CRM and product data during setup at no additional cost.

Is there an API?

Yes, all plans include read access to our REST API, with write access and higher rate limits available on Growth and Enterprise.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing is based on the number of seats and which integrations you enable. Contact sales for a custom Enterprise quote.

Do you support multi-currency reporting?

Yes, Growth and Enterprise plans support multi-currency pipeline and revenue reporting with daily FX rate updates.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export all of your data at any time, and we retain it for 30 days after cancellation before permanent deletion.

Can I customize which fields sync?

Yes, field-level mapping lets you choose exactly which CRM and product fields sync into Synapse, and in which direction.

Do you offer onboarding support?

Every paid plan includes guided onboarding; Enterprise customers get a dedicated implementation manager for the first 90 days.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes, Synapse is available on iOS and Android with full access to pipeline, alerts, and account health scores.
